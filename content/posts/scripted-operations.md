The [Missions](/rules) book has everything you need to generate and play your own missions. But for players who want a little more flavor, I wrote up 36 three-Mission Operations for PvE play.

These operations match your Squad's Faction to each other Faction (including "mirror" matchups, e.g. Hegemony vs Hegemony).

### Design

For variety of gameplay, I wrote these with the following constraints:

- Each Mission in a given Operation has a Primary Objective of a distinct Archetype with a distinct assigned Deployment
  - You still roll the Secondary Objective normally, but it has to be of a different Archetype than the Primary Objective
- Each Operation has an assigned PvE Squad for your Squad to face, and an assigned Battlefield. Both are shared throughout all three Missions in the Operation.
- The narrative is kept short by design: a few lines per Operation and Mission, just enough to give your Squad a reason to fight the good fight.
