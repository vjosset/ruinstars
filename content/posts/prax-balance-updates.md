The **[Praxium Corps](/squadTypes/PRX)** were always intended to be a high-skill, high-lethality Squad. But as we played missions, we realized a few things:

- They're generally overpowered
- Their skills and specialisms didn't always fit their lore and how we wanted them to feel on the tabletop

So the following changes have been applied to the Praxium Corps:

- Removed "Duty Before Death" - No squad specialty for Prax (they're individuals)
  - It was a duplicate of Interdictors anyway
- All Unit Types at 5 HIT; **Leader** no longer gives `+1 HIT`
- Only Melee units get Counter; removed from Ranged units; moved Counter to Skills instead of Squad Specialty
- Aegis:
  - Removed Aggressor (pistol)
  - Aegis Aura applies only for Melee combat and only to Squadmates, not self
- Vorax:
  - Clarified Jump Strike gives a _free_ Melee attack
- Vector:
  - Scan is once per mission (or Horde Wave), not per Turn
  - Removed Counter
- Wraith:
  - Removed Counter
- Pillar:
  - Replace Formation with Concentrated Fire: gives a Ranged Combat attack re-roll to Squadmates only, not self
  - Removed Counter
- Raptor:
  - No changes

---

These updates are live now in the app and on the faction pages. As always, the rules are free, the squads are yours to build, and any feedback helps.
