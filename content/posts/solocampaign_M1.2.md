### Operation 1 - Mission 1.2

_2026-04-01_

![Mission Start](/blog/images/solocampaign_M1.2.jpg)

> - **Battlefield:** The Cursed Temple (Haunting Spirits)
> - **Enemy Squad:** Hunter Killers - 6 Killers (Melee)
> - **Deployment:** Deep Strike
> - **Objective A:** Destroy var 3 (2 of 3, regen)
> - **Objective B:** Control var 2 (2 of 3, two consecutive turns)

#### Summary

- Destroy objective completed T1
- Control objective completed T3
- Casualties:
  - Castius: Taken Out T1, received Crushed Leg injury
  - Decaeta: Taken Out T2, received Damaged Armor injury
  - NPC Squad: Eliminated in T3
- All objectives completed + No survivors &rarr; **4 MP**

#### Notes

- 30 minutes again, I think that's solid. I can picture doing a full 3-mission operation in an afternoon/evening
- It's tough to remember NPC specials like Acid Blood for the Killers (like Arise! for the Sentinels in mission 1.1)
- Got lucky with very strong shots on the "Destroy" objectives and poor save rolls in T1
- Got lucky with the Haunting Spirits
  - One Killer killed another one
  - Corvius shot Andronicos three times really hard, but Andronicos got lucky on save rolls with TO assists and they're still friends
- NPC behavior "Ignore increased ACT cost for repeated actions" is brutal, especially allowing melee units to cover half the board if needed
- Board was pretty open giving an advantage to Ranged Units, but the Killers were moving fast.
- With the injuries on the squad, Mission 1.3 could be difficult:
  - Corvius: Deep Wound
  - Castius: Crushed Leg
  - Decaeta: Deep Wound + Damaged Armor (she's not going to last long next mission)
