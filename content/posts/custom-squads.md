Each of the factions in _Ruinstars_ defines a clear identity and a distinct
approach to getting the job done. But you may have minis that don't fit any of
them. Until now your options were to proxy them into an established squad, or
leave them collecting dust on the shelf.

So I've added a [Custom](/squadTypes/CUS) squad type. Pick a chassis for each
Unit: Light, Medium or Heavy, in a Melee or Ranged role, then spend your GP
across 18 weapons, 29 skills and 8 new Squad Specialties. Six chassis, and
whatever you want to build on top of them.

## How it's costed

The chassis Unit stat lines are drawn from published Unit Types, so a Custom squad
costs roughly what an equivalent canon squad would. Weapons are priced off standard
medians, and skills are all flat 2 GP.

That last one is deliberate: I could have graded them, but nothing here has been
played yet, and a precise-looking price list would only be pretending. Flat is
the honest starting point.

So: Treat these as a starting point, not a balanced alternative to the standard
squads. They aren't tuned against each other, against canon squads, or against
NPC and Horde forces.

I hope this fills a gap for the minis you want to get on the battlefield!

As always, feedback is welcome and appreciated!
