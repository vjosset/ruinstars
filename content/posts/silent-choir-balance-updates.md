Our last update gave every **[Silent Choir](/squadTypes/SCH)** unit tier more weapons and skills to choose from. Good for build variety, but it also pushed the whole squad's numbers up further than we intended, and blurred the line between what a Disciple is for and what a Shroudbearer is for.

So the following changes have been applied to the Silent Choir:

- **Relic Blade** adjusted across all units: `ATT 4 -> 3`, cost `4 GP -> 3 GP`
- **Relic Lash** reworked: no longer a 2GP, squad-wide Melee skill. It's now a Unique weapon (`Relic Lash*`, Rending 1, 4 GP), available only to Shroudbearer, Adept, and Disciple.
- **Mind Spike** cost increased: `4 GP -> 5 GP`
- **Dark Shard\*** reworked: Ranged weapons now gain Relentless instead of Rending 1
- **HIT** reduced: Shroudbearer `5 -> 4`, Aspirant `5 -> 4`, Adept `4 -> 3`
- **Adept ARM** reduced: `4 -> 3`

---

These updates are live now in the app and on the [faction page](/squadTypes/SCH). As always, the [rules](/rules) are free, the squads are yours to build, and any feedback helps.
